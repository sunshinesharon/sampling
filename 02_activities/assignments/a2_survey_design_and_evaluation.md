## Part B - Survey Evaluation:

Identify and describe survey features:

```
Sample Type - Stratified probability sample with a cross-sectional design.

Sample Size - It includes approx 50,000 units with 40,000 Invitation letters sent. The expected completion was around 20,000 questionnaires.

Target Population - Everyone ages 15 and above, living across the 10 provinces in Canada, excluding full-time residents of institutions.

Sampling Frame: This survey uses a frame that combines landline and cellular telephone numbers from the Census and various administrative sources with Statistics Canada's dwelling frame.

Survey Mode: a. Electronic Questionnaire b. Computer-assisted telephone interviewing

Timeline: September to December 2018 (every 5 years)
Tentative Release Date - Jan 26, 2021

Response Rate: 40% (20,000/50,000)

Weights: The survey uses a 'weighing factor' to ensure the estimates match the 2017 CIS income distribution by province.

Data Processing:
- Automatic and manual edits performed at different stages (macro and micro levels).  
- Family, consistency, and flow edits used to ensure data integrity.  
- Errors detected through the CATI system were resolved immediately when possible.

Cleaning, Imputation: 
- Donor imputation used to fill missing responses based on records with similar characteristics.  
- Mean imputation was used when donor imputation was not feasible.  
- Missing income data were retrieved from linked tax records where consent was given.

Sources of Error:
- Sampling error: Estimates may vary across different samples.  
- Non-sampling error: Imperfect coverage, non-response bias, response errors, and processing errors.  
- Coverage error: Households without telephones were excluded, which may introduce bias.

Known Biases & Limitations:
- Differences in results compared to previous survey cycles may be due to changes in methodology rather than actual shifts in population behavior.  
- Households without telephones were excluded, which may impact representativeness.  
- Use of an online survey option in 2018 could have influenced response patterns.  
- Efforts were made to minimize non-response bias by adjusting weights.

Link to the document I referenced: https://www23.statcan.gc.ca/imdb/p2SV.pl?Function=getSurvey&Id=796234

```
